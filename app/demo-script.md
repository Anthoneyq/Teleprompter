# One useful next step

Take a breath before you begin.

## Start small

You do not need to change everything today.

Choose **one useful action** you can repeat.

- Make the next step clear.
- Make it manageable.
- Give yourself time to practise.

> Pause here. Look up.

## Make it specific

Instead of saying, “I will be more consistent,” decide what you will do next and when you will do it.

A clear plan gives you something you can actually follow.
