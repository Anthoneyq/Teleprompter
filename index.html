How it works

Three steps, then you are reading.

  1. Paste your script.Plain text works. Headings, bullet points and bold help you see where you are in the script.
  2. Set a pace you can speak to.Pick the scroll speed, text size and line spacing. A guide line keeps your place. Mirror modes are there if you read off glass.
  3. Tap to start. Tap to stop.Tap the script or press Space. Nudge the speed with − and + while you read. Reset takes you back to the top.

It does not

Questions

Before you buy.

What do I need to run it?

A current desktop browser. Unzip the whole folder and open index.html. Keep the files together. [Supported browsers and systems: set after the owner device rehearsal.]

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

The free browser version runs on phones and tablets and has been checked in iPhone and iPad simulators. The download is meant for a computer. [Phone and tablet support is promised only after a real-device rehearsal.]

Where are my scripts kept?

In your browser on your device. Clearing browser data or using private browsing can remove them, so use Export backup for anything you want to keep.

Can I use it with prompter glass?

There are horizontal and vertical mirror modes. Specific glass and remotes have not been tested.
